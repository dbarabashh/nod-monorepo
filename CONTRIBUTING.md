# Contributing

Nod is open source, but it doesn't take code contributions: pull requests
aren't accepted. All of the code is written by one person, Dima Barabash, who
holds its copyright. That keeps the licensing simple and his to change.

Issues are welcome. If something is broken, confusing, or missing, open an
issue and say:

- what you did,
- what you expected,
- what happened instead,
- and which app and version (desktop on macOS, Windows or Linux, iPhone,
  Android, or the sync relay).

Please don't paste meeting content, transcripts, recovery phrases, pairing
codes, identity cards or relay tokens into an issue. Describe them instead.

Security problems go by email to the address in `SECURITY.md` once it exists,
or to the maintainer directly, not into a public issue.

You're free to fork Nod under its licenses (see `README.md`). A fork must not
use the name "Nod" or its logo in a way that suggests it is the official app;
see `NOTICE`.
